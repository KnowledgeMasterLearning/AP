Course Overview

AP ENVIRONMENTAL SCIENCE · COURSE OVERVIEW

AP Environmental Science is divided into 9 units, built around ecosystems, biodiversity, populations, earth systems and resources, energy consumption and pollution, with a focus on the interactions between the natural environment and human activity.

Units and Exam Weighting

Unit 1The Living World: Ecosystems6–8%
Unit 2The Living World: Biodiversity6–8%
Unit 3Populations10–15%
Unit 4Earth Systems and Resources10–15%
Unit 5Land and Water Use10–15%
Unit 6Energy Resources and Consumption10–15%
Unit 7Atmospheric Pollution7–10%
Unit 8Aquatic and Terrestrial Pollution7–10%
Unit 9Global Change15–20%
Note: Unit 9 (Global Change) carries the highest weight and serves as the course's capstone. Units 3, 4, 5, and 6 are tied for second — all key units.
