Samuel

Knowledge Master · Founder

Hi, I'm Samuel. Knowledge Master is a study platform I founded. It gathers the AP course outlines, exam focus points, concept summaries, and official CEDs I've organized, plus past AP exams and CB question banks I've been able to collect — all shared for free, so that more students can learn AP with ease.

I've taken 15 AP exams, and I can say with confidence — AP is not hard to learn. The content is challenging, the pace is fast, and everything is in English, which does trip up many students. But with the right approach, every problem can be solved. There are two most common issues: first, students can't find past exams or question banks; second, the material is dense and the language barrier makes it hard to keep up, leading to giving up. Both of these can actually be avoided.

This is exactly why I founded this platform: to offer one-on-one AP online courses, "spoon-feeding" the concepts to students — adjusting the pace to their absorption, teaching bilingually to ensure understanding, reporting to parents before and after class, and answering questions anytime. Several students have already improved because of this. Passing on what I've learned to more people is itself a meaningful thing.
